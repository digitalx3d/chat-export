Chat Brief

Privacy policy

Chat Brief, a Chrome add-on by Digital-X, LLC (Arizona, USA). Last updated: 21 October 2026.

The short version: Chat Brief turns the AI chat in front of you into a file on your computer. Your chat is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics.

What the add-on reads

Chat Brief reads a page only when you ask it to: you click its toolbar button, or press Alt+Shift+E, on a ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini chat. It reads that one tab, once. It cannot see other tabs or other websites, and it does nothing in the background while you browse.

From that tab it reads:

  • the messages in the conversation, including code, tables, links and images;
  • the chat's title and web address, which go in the header of your file.

What happens to your chat

Everything happens on your computer:

  1. The add-on collects the conversation from the page.
  2. It holds the conversation in your browser's memory for the few seconds it takes to open the export screen. That copy is deleted as soon as the export screen picks it up, or after two minutes, whichever comes first.
  3. You choose Markdown or PDF. The file is created on your computer and saved where your browser saves downloads. PDF files are made by Chrome's own "Save as PDF".

The add-on does not keep a copy of your chat. It does not save a history of what you exported. It stores nothing on your computer or in your Google account; the only thing it leaves behind is the file you chose to save.

What leaves your computer

Your chat does not. Nothing you type or read in a chat is sent to us, and we have no server to send it to.

One thing does use the internet: images. If the chat shows images, the add-on downloads them again from the same place the chat page loads them from (the chat service or its image host), so they can be placed inside your file. These are the same requests the page itself makes to show you those images. They carry no part of your conversation, and they go to the chat service you are already using, never to us.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no crash reports, no account. We do not know who uses Chat Brief or what they export.

Because we collect nothing, we sell nothing and share nothing. Our use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Other companies

  • The chat services. OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude) and Google (Gemini) run their own sites under their own privacy policies. Chat Brief does not change what they collect.
  • Google, as the Chrome Web Store. Installing and updating the add-on goes through Google's Chrome Web Store, under Google's privacy policy.

Chat Brief is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Anthropic or Google.

Children

Chat Brief is not directed at children under 13, and it collects nothing from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the add-on ever starts handling information differently, we will update this page before that version is released, and change the date at the top.
