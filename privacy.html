ChatFiler

Privacy policy

ChatFiler, a Chrome add-on that saves your AI chats as files, by Digital-X, LLC (Arizona, USA). Last updated: 21 October 2026.

The short version: ChatFiler turns the AI chat in front of you into a file on your computer. Your chat is never sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics.

What the add-on reads

ChatFiler reads a page only when you ask it to: you click its toolbar button, or press Alt+Shift+E, on a ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini chat. It reads that one tab, once. It cannot see other tabs or other websites, and it does nothing in the background while you browse.

From that tab it reads:

  • the messages in the conversation, including code, tables, links and images;
  • the chat's title and web address, which go in the header of your file.

What happens to your chat

Everything happens on your computer:

  1. The add-on collects the conversation from the page.
  2. It holds the conversation in your browser's memory for the few seconds it takes to open the export screen. That copy is deleted as soon as the export screen picks it up, or after two minutes, whichever comes first.
  3. If the Brief is switched on, the add-on lists your questions at the top of the file and, if you ask for one, has Chrome's built-in AI write a short summary (see the next section).
  4. You choose Markdown or PDF. The file is created on your computer and saved where your browser saves downloads. PDF files are made by Chrome's own "Save as PDF".

The add-on does not keep a copy of your chat. It does not save a history of what you exported. It stores nothing on your computer or in your Google account; the only thing it leaves behind is the file you chose to save.

The Brief and Chrome's built-in AI

The summary in the Brief is written by Chrome's built-in AI, which runs on your computer. The add-on passes your conversation to it inside your browser. No part of your chat is sent to us, to Google or to anyone else to write it.

The first time you ask for a summary, Chrome may need to download its AI model from Google, a few gigabytes. The add-on asks first, with a "Download and write the summary" button. That download is Chrome fetching its own model; it carries none of your conversation. The model belongs to Chrome, not to ChatFiler, and Chrome manages it under Google's privacy policy. If you'd rather not download it, the Brief still lists your questions without it.

What leaves your computer

Your chat does not. Nothing you type or read in a chat is sent to us, and we have no server to send it to.

Two things do use the internet, and neither carries any part of your conversation:

  • Images. If the chat shows images, the add-on downloads them again from the same place the chat page loads them from (the chat service or its image host), so they can be placed inside your file. These are the same requests the page itself makes to show you those images, and they go to the chat service you are already using, never to us.
  • Chrome's AI model, the one-time download from Google described above, and only if you ask for a summary.

What we collect

Nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no crash reports, no account. We do not know who uses ChatFiler or what they export.

Because we collect nothing, we sell nothing and share nothing. Our use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Other companies

  • The chat services. OpenAI (ChatGPT), Anthropic (Claude) and Google (Gemini) run their own sites under their own privacy policies. ChatFiler does not change what they collect.
  • Google, as the Chrome Web Store and as Chrome. Installing and updating the add-on goes through Google's Chrome Web Store, and Chrome's built-in AI is part of Chrome. Both are covered by Google's privacy policy.

ChatFiler is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Anthropic or Google.

Children

ChatFiler is not directed at children under 13, and it collects nothing from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the add-on ever starts handling information differently, we will update this page before that version is released, and change the date at the top.
